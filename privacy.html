Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

AI Golf Coach (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website. By using AI Golf Coach, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Who We Are

AI Golf Coach is a Canadian golf coaching application powered by Ace, your personal AI golf professional. We are based in Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada and operate under Canadian federal privacy law (PIPEDA).

For privacy inquiries: aigolfcoach@proton.me

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account we collect your name, email address, and password (encrypted). You may optionally provide your age range, dominant hand, handicap, experience level, typical score, play frequency, and golf goals. You may sign in using Apple Sign In or Google Sign In, in which case we receive basic profile information from those providers.

Golf Performance Data

We collect golf data you enter including round scores, course names, fairways hit, greens in regulation, putts, birdies, pars, bogeys, shot-by-shot logging data, and other scoring statistics. We also collect launch monitor data including club speed, ball speed, launch angle, spin rate, carry distance, and related metrics. Shot tracking and ball flight visualisation data is collected when you use those features.

Swing Videos, Photos, and Uploaded Media

With your permission, we collect swing videos and photos you record or upload for analysis by Ace Studio. These files are stored securely in Cloudflare R2 cloud storage and are associated with your account and accessible only to you. Uploaded media is used solely to provide swing analysis and coaching feedback.

We do not use uploaded swing videos or photos to train AI models. We do not share them with third parties except as required to operate the cloud storage service.

GPS Round Tracking Data

When you use the GPS Round Tracker feature, we collect your precise GPS location approximately every 5 seconds throughout your round. This includes latitude, longitude, timestamp, and accuracy data. Location tracking continues in the background when the app is minimised during an active round.

GPS data is stored locally on your device during the round, uploaded to our servers when you save the round, used only to generate your round replay map and calculate distances, never shared with third parties, and deleted when you delete the round or your account.

You can revoke location permissions at any time in your device settings, which will disable the GPS round tracker feature. Location data is not collected when a round is not actively being tracked.

Games Data (Wolf, Skins, Matchplay)

When you use the live scoring game features, we collect game session data including scores, game type, participating player display names, hole-by-hole results, and game outcomes. This data is stored with your account and is not shared publicly.

Practice and Fitness Data

We collect practice session data including drill history, completed exercises, fitness screening results, workout session duration, and mental game session history.

Social and Community Data (The Clubhouse)

When you use The Clubhouse social features, we collect posts you create, comments, likes, and follow relationships. All posts in The Clubhouse are visible to all registered users of AI Golf Coach by default — there is no private posting mode. You control whether your Locker (golf profile statistics) is visible to other users. Content you post remains visible to other users until you delete it.

In-App Notifications

We send in-app notifications for activity related to your account, such as new followers and replies to your comments. These notifications appear within the app only and are not push notifications sent to your device when the app is closed.

Device and Analytics Information

We automatically collect device type, operating system, and app version. We use Expo Analytics to collect crash reports, screen view data, and session information to help us identify and fix technical issues and understand how the app is used. This data is developer-facing only — it is used internally to improve the app and is not shared with third parties or used for advertising. We do not use third-party advertising analytics or tracking pixels. Our website uses only functional cookies necessary to operate the site and does not use tracking or marketing cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide personalised AI golf coaching through Ace
  • Analyse your golf performance and generate coaching insights
  • Process and display uploaded swing videos for Ace Studio analysis
  • Generate your round replay map from GPS tracking data
  • Power live scoring for Wolf, Skins, and Matchplay games
  • Save and retrieve your golf stats, sessions, and history
  • Power The Clubhouse social community features
  • Send in-app notifications for social activity on your account
  • Identify and fix technical issues using crash and usage analytics
  • Improve our app features and user experience
  • Respond to customer support requests
  • Ensure app security and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your golf data, swing videos, or personal information to train AI models. We do not sell your data. We do not display advertising.

Our legal bases for processing your data are: performance of our contract with you (providing the service), our legitimate interests in improving the app and preventing fraud, your consent where required (camera, location), and compliance with legal obligations.

4. Background Location Collection

AI Golf Coach collects your location in the background ONLY when you have actively started a GPS round tracking session.

Background location collection only occurs during an active tracked round — it automatically stops when you end the round, is clearly indicated in the app with an active round indicator, uses approximately 8–12% battery over a 4-hour round, and can be stopped at any time by ending the round or revoking location permissions.

We never collect background location outside of active round tracking sessions. iOS users will see a blue status bar indicator when background location is active. We request “Always” location permission solely to support uninterrupted round tracking when the app is backgrounded during a round.

5. AI Coaching and Third-Party Services

Ace AI Coaching System

Our AI coaching feature uses a tiered approach. Free tier coaching insights — including Ace Studio swing analysis up to the monthly session limit — are generated by our proprietary rules engine, entirely on your device, with zero data sent to external servers. Premium tier coaching without BYOK uses the same on-device rules engine as the free tier — no external data transmission.

Bring Your Own Key (BYOK): Premium users may optionally provide their own Anthropic API key to enable live AI coaching. When BYOK is active, your golf profile, launch session data, and round history are sent directly from your device to Anthropic to generate coaching responses. Your API key is stored encrypted on your device only — we never see, store, or transmit your key. All AI calls go device → Anthropic directly, bypassing our servers entirely. No GPS data, swing videos, or payment information is ever sent to Anthropic. You are responsible for all costs incurred through your key. Anthropic’s privacy policy applies to data they receive. BYOK is entirely optional — the app is fully functional without it.

Expo Analytics

We use Expo Analytics to collect app crash reports, performance data, and anonymised usage statistics. This data is used internally by our development team to identify bugs and improve the app. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or shared with third parties.

Supabase

We use Supabase to store your account data, golf statistics, game scores, social posts, and app data. Your data is stored on secure servers with industry-standard encryption and row-level security policies that ensure only you can access your own data.

Cloudflare R2

Swing videos and photos you upload are stored in Cloudflare R2 cloud storage with access controls tied to your account. Cloudflare’s privacy policy applies to this storage service.

Golf Course API

Our GPS caddie feature uses a third-party Golf Course API to retrieve course layout information. No personal data is shared with this service.

Apple Sign In / Google Sign In

If you use Sign In with Apple or Google Sign In, those providers share basic profile information with us (name and email). Their respective privacy policies govern the data they collect from you.

10. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the app via the drawer menu → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes all personal data, golf statistics, swing videos, game history, social posts, and AI coaching history.

  • Swing videos and photos deleted from Cloudflare R2 immediately
  • All remaining data purged within 30 days
  • GPS round data, game scores, social posts, and AI history deleted immediately

To request deletion by email: aigolfcoach@proton.me

11. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, personal data is deleted within 30 days except where legally required to retain it. Swing videos, GPS data, game data, social posts, and AI analysis history are deleted immediately upon account deletion or individual deletion requests.
